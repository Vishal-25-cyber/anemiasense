How It Works

Two distinct, independently trained deep learning pipelines designed for non-invasive clinical evaluation

Eye Conjunctiva Model

MobileNetV2 Dual-Head
Architecture
MobileNetV2 Multi-Task Network
Input Shape
224 × 224 × 3 RGB
Normalization
External: (pixel / 127.5) − 1.0 → [-1, 1]
Head 1 Output
Anemia Risk Probability ∈ [0, 1]
Head 2 Output
Estimated Hemoglobin (g/dL)
Dataset
217 Patients across two international cohorts
Performance
AUC: 0.838 · Hb MAE ≈ 1.95 g/dL

Features Grad-CAM visual attention heatmaps calculated on the final convolutional layer (out_relu) to confirm localization of palpebral mucosa vascularity.

Nail Bed Model

MobileNetV3Large
Architecture
MobileNetV3Large + GAP + Dense(1)
Input Shape
224 × 224 × 3 RGB
Normalization
Internal Rescaling Layer (1/127.5, offset -1.0)
Model Output
Direct Hemoglobin Regression (g/dL)
Classification
Derived from WHO demographic thresholds

Receives raw RGB float pixels [0, 255]. The model handles feature scaling internally. Because it is a regression-only model, severity categories are determined directly from the estimated hemoglobin level.

Clinical & Research Disclaimer

  • AnemiaSense is an experimental research tool and is not an FDA-approved or CE-cleared diagnostic medical device.
  • Digital non-invasive hemoglobin estimates carry optical uncertainties caused by device sensors, camera compression, and lighting conditions.
  • Always confirm any abnormal screening score with a certified venipuncture laboratory Complete Blood Count (CBC) and serum ferritin test.
  • Never alter iron supplements, dietary therapies, or prescribed medical treatment based solely on non-invasive app outputs.
  • If experiencing symptoms such as severe breathlessness, lightheadedness, chest pain, or tachycardia, seek urgent medical care immediately.
